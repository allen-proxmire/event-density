- **Generalized Einstein-aether** (Zlosnik–Ferreira–Starkman 2007): replacing the aether's canonical kinetic term with a nonlinear function of the kinetic scalar yields, in the static weak-field limit, a **modified Poisson equation of exactly the MOND form**, with the interpolation function determined by the derivative of the kinetic function and the transition scale set by the scale inside it.

**Dictionary statement.** Writing the khronon sector's low-energy kinetic function as `W(X)` (symbol chosen to avoid collision with the dynamical-bandwidth rule `F`), with `X ∼ |∇χ|²/a₀²` the scalar's gradient invariant against the transition scale, the static weak-field limit gives

> `∇·[ μ(|∇Φ|/a₀) ∇Φ ] = 4πGρ`, with `μ = W′(X)`.

The two regimes map as: **`W′ → 1` (canonical kinetics) ⟺ `μ → 1` ⟺ Newtonian/GR recovery**, and **`W′(X) = √X` ⟺ `μ(x) = x` ⟺ the deep-MOND "simple" interpolation**, i.e. `W(X) = (2/3)X^{3/2}` in the deep IR. [All of §2: **I — external**, the arc's standing dictionary.]

**What is forced, and given what.** The corpus has *already derived* the deep-MOND law by a substrate route: Paper_030's Combination Rule `a = √(a_N a₀)` (P14 bilocal coupling) — which is exactly the simple interpolation `μ(x) = x`. Matching the khronometric EFT to that *already-derived* law fixes the deep-IR kinetic function **uniquely**: `μ = W′(X) = √X ⟹ W = (2/3)X^{3/2}`. So:

> **`W` is FORCED-GIVEN-030** — uniquely determined by matching a law the substrate program independently derived — **not forced from the primitives alone.** The khronon does not re-derive the geometric-mean law; it *embeds it relativistically*. This is the same epistemic shape as GR-I inheriting Newton: the khronon-MOND construction is the **relativistic completion** of the flat-space substrate law, not its origin.

**What would upgrade it.** A primitives-level account of *why* the commitment/bandwidth dynamics go sub-linear below the cosmic rate — i.e., a derivation of the geometric-mean crossover from the substrate side (presumably the regime where local bandwidth gradients fall beneath the khronon's background rate, so P-Commitment-Linear's regime assumption degrades). That is flagged as the deeper open item and **deliberately not attempted here** — it is exactly where reverse-engineering would masquerade as derivation. Until then: forced-given-030, and labeled so.

**The retrofit guard.** The interpolating function *between* the two fixed asymptotics (`W′→1` large `X`; `W′=√X` small `X`) remains a family — the same residual-family status the corpus already assigns to the MOND interpolation `μ(x)` (Papers 030/033/035). Nothing here pins it, and no choice within it is load-bearing for the unification claim.
