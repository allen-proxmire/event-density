## 1. The substrate d.o.f. for the spinor

From the channel concept (P07):

- **A channel is "a direction a chain can keep going in"** — a stable, directional, rule-type-selective pathway in the participation graph (P07 §1). Channels are countable and compose (branch/merge).

- **Spin and gauge structure are channel *topology*:** P07 §7.4 — "gauge-like phases arise from non-trivial channel topology … showing that **U(1), SU(2), SU(3) arise as specific channel-topology classes** is the route to the ED account of gauge theory. Phase 2/3 target." And §4 lists "topological mode counting (Chern numbers) … count classes of channels distinguished by participation-graph topology."

So the substrate has exactly the pieces step 1 predicted: a **directional/topological** structure (channels) and a **U(1) phase** (P09) riding on it.
