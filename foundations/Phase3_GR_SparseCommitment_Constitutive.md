## 1. Dense commitment abolishes quantum mechanics — rigorously (quantum Zeno)

If commitment (P11) fired at every tick (`P11 ≡ P13`), every degree of freedom would be *measured* (indeterminate → determinate) every Planck time. This is exactly the **quantum Zeno limit**: continuous measurement does not merely weaken interference — it **freezes evolution entirely.** A continuously-measured system cannot evolve; it is pinned to its instantaneous eigenstate. So the dense branch gives:

- **no superposition** (collapsed every tick),
- **no interference** (no coherent phase survives a tick),
- **no coherence** (off-diagonal terms destroyed continuously),
- **no unitary evolution** (irreversible collapse every tick),
- and, via Zeno, **no dynamics at all.**

ED has a quantum sector (P09: superposition, interference, the U(1) phase). Therefore the dense branch is not "disfavored" — it is **incompatible with ED's existence.** Sparse commitment is the *only* branch on which ED has quantum mechanics.

## 2. ED's structure *is* the quantum-measurement structure

On the sparse branch, ED's dynamics are: **unitary becoming** (the P09 phase evolving reversibly, in proper time) **punctuated by rare irreversible commitments** (P11, indeterminate → determinate). That is precisely the textbook quantum-measurement picture — unitary Schrödinger evolution interrupted by occasional, irreversible collapse. So in ED:

> **commitment (P11) = the quantum measurement / decoherence event**, and it must be *sparse* for the same reason measurements are rare in quantum mechanics — continuous measurement (Zeno) would freeze everything. ED's irreversible arrow and its quantum sector are the *same structure* seen from two sides: the arrow is the collapse, the sparseness is what leaves room for unitary evolution between collapses.

## 3. The same structure gives gravitational time dilation

Unitary becoming proceeds in **proper time**, and proper time runs at a position-dependent rate — the lapse `N` (`N² ∼ b`). **Gravitational time dilation just is becoming proceeding at different rates in different places** (Allen's observation: "it's hard to have time dilation if it ticks the same"). A uniform, dense, every-tick commitment would tick the same everywhere — no lapse variation, no time dilation. The *non-uniformity* of becoming — the very thing that makes commitment sparse and matter-tracking — is what makes proper time, and hence time dilation, possible. So:

> **the position-dependent rate of becoming is simultaneously (i) the lapse of General Relativity (time dilation) and (ii) a face of the non-uniform, sparse commitment that quantum mechanics requires.** GR's clock and QM's coherence are the same becoming-structure.
