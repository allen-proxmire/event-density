# GR α₁ band-partition — does it rest on the archived four-band P04 structure?

**Run:** 2026-07-06, following the mass↔GR sparse-commitment cross-connection investigation (`theory/Higgs_Emergence/Mass_GR_SparseCommitment_CrossConnection.md`), which found that the 2026-07-05 `FourBand_Dependency_Check.md` cleared the *mass* sector of the four-band dependency but never covered the Phase-3 GR α₁ band-partition work — where the bands are load-bearing, not decorative.

## Verdict: SUBSTANTIVELY CANONICAL. Presentationally over-claims (same debt as the mass sector). The α₁ safety result does NOT rest on the archived structure.

The GR α₁ mechanism's load-bearing content is the contrast between two bands — a density-independent metric stiffness `M_P²` and a density-dependent khronon stiffness `f²`. Checked directly how the two load-bearing documents source those two bands:

**The load-bearing measurement (`Phase3_GR_BandPartition_TargetA_Findings.md`, the "c₂ = sparsity MEASURED" result) sources both bands from CANONICAL primitive dynamics, not from the archived four-band primitive claim** (verbatim, §Setup):
> "an **always-on adjacency** band (**P02 symmetric share**, fraction s₀₂=0.20 every tick → M_P²), and a **sparse commitment-reserve** (**P11 commitment events** at rate ρ_event, finite per-locus capacity CAP=8 → the foliation/khronon stiffness f²)."

So the measured result — `M_P²` density-independent (3.1% flat), `f² ∝ ρ_event` (linear, slope 1.14) — comes from the *dynamics* of two genuinely distinct **canonical** primitives:
- `M_P²` (metric stiffness): **P02** participation-sharing, always-on every tick → density-independent *because* P02 sharing happens with or without commitments. Canonical.
- `f²` (khronon stiffness): **P11** commitment events, sparse/event-based → density-dependent *because* it scales with how many commitments fire (`ρ_event`). Canonical.

The other two of the "four bands" are explicitly **not load-bearing for α₁**: the internal band is just the chain's own participation (the matter source, canonical P02); the environmental band is "the decoherence/measurement sink (background; not load-bearing for α₁)" (`Phase3_GR_BandPartition_Scoping.md`, §1 table). So the α₁-relevant content collapses cleanly to a **P02-vs-P11 decomposition**, both canonical primitives — exactly the "fine" reading the cross-connection investigation hypothesized, now confirmed.

## The presentational debt (same as the mass sector, same fix)

Both GR band docs still *frame* the split as the archived four-band partition:
- `Phase3_GR_BandPartition_Scoping.md` §1 labels its table "Band (P04 §1.5)" and calls for "the **explicit P04 four-band partition**."
- `Phase3_GR_BandPartition_TargetA_Findings.md` §Keystone says the scaling is "measured from the **four-band dynamics**."

"P04 §1.5 / four-band" is the archived, non-canonical citation (canonical P04 is a bare additive scalar; the four-band split traces to the retired M-series forcing paper, per `FourBand_Dependency_Check.md`). So the GR work carries the **same presentational over-claim** the mass sector did — citing the four bands as if they were Primitive 04 — even though the load-bearing substance rests on canonical P02 vs P11.

**Fix (presentation, not results), identical in spirit to the mass-sector fix:** in the Phase-3 GR band-partition docs, either (a) re-frame the two load-bearing bands as a canonical **P02-always-on-sharing vs P11-sparse-commitment** decomposition (which is what the sims actually implement), noting any four-band vocabulary is optional bookkeeping; or (b) keep the band vocabulary but flag it explicitly as a non-canonical organizing refinement that no α₁ result depends on. Either restores honest primitive sourcing without touching the α₁ safety verdict.
