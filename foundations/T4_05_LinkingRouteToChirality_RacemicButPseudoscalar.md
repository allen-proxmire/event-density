## 1. Why linking is a *better* γ⁵ candidate than the screw
γ⁵ (chirality) is a **pseudoscalar**: parity-odd (it changes sign under spatial reflection). For a substrate feature to *be* γ⁵ it must itself be a genuine pseudoscalar. Two candidates:
- **The P05-transport screw (T4_04):** its handedness = `n_z`, the frame-rotation *about the arrow*. But the arrow is a *time* axis and picks out no spatial rotation axis, so `n_z` is unforced → the screw is not even present by default, let alone handed. **A non-starter (T4_04's finding).**
- **The multi-chain V5 linking (this note):** the **linking number of two threaded chains is a true topological pseudoscalar** — a right-handed link and its mirror image have opposite linking number. And ED's multi-chain participation graph is **intrinsically linked in 3D** (the `ChainsAsLinks_MultichainV5_Reachable` K6-minor result). So unlike the screw, a genuine parity-odd invariant is *present and topologically guaranteed* in ED's own structure. **This is the strongest γ⁵ candidate the substrate offers, and T4_04 did not check it.**

## 2. Does it give *net* chirality? No — racemic, for a clean reason
A γ⁵ needs a **net** pseudoscalar (a systematic excess of one handedness). The linking ensemble has a net sign only if the rules that *generate* the links carry a parity-odd bias. They do not:
- **P09 polarity is parity-even** (an internal U(1) circle; parity does not act on it — the standing #2b result).
- **The arrow is time-odd, not space-odd** (it breaks T, not P; it picks no spatial handedness — the same fact behind T4_04 forks a/b).
- **The V5 coupling is handedness-blind** (the homochirality Probe 2: the real V5 functional `E=Σ e^{−r/ℓ}cos Δφ` gave `κ_h≈0`, geometry/handedness-blind — no same-vs-opposite-handed preference).

With no parity-odd ingredient in the link-generating rules, the ensemble of V5 links is **racemic** — equal populations of `+` and `−` linking, net pseudoscalar zero. So the linking route gives **no native net γ⁵**: the emergent fermion is vector-like, exactly as the other routes found. **Fifth independent confirmation** that ED's substrate does not natively produce weak parity violation (screw, canonical-P07, first-arrival, homochirality-`κ_h`, and now multi-chain linking). *Honest tier: this is the parity-symmetry argument applied to the linking route + Probe 2, not a fresh linking-sign measurement — a direct one would hit the `ChainsAsLinks_OperationalTest` tooling wall (clean linking numbers were not extractable from the embeddings), so the argument, not a new probe, carries it.*

So the question:

**Honest tier + why it is not a result:** this is a *sharpened open sub-question*, explicitly **not** a claim that ED reopens parity violation.
