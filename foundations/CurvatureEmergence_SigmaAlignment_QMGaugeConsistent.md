## 1. The risk

The quadratic-strain recast (`CurvatureEmergence_QuadraticStrainRecast_...`) needs local participation phases to carry a small common bias `σ = |⟨e^{iπ}⟩|` toward the horizon reference, so that local-local coherence is `σ²` (Newton-safe) and local-horizon coherence is `σ` (MOND). A universal preferred phase is exactly the kind of thing that could break the QM / gauge sector: it could (a) make the global phase observable / break global U(1) invariance, or (b) shift double-slit interference (which uses the same `2√(b_A b_B)cosΔπ` object). If it does, the recast's mechanism is dead. This note runs the check before building further.

## 2. Why it is consistent: σ is a relative phase to a physical structure

The resolution is the distinction between *absolute* and *relative* phase, applied carefully. QM and gauge physics depend only on *relative* phases. And `σ` is defined relative to the **horizon**, which in ED is a *physical* global structure (the SCBU boundary, the A1 capacity surface), not an arbitrary gauge choice. So `σ = |⟨e^{i(π_i - π_{hor})}⟩|` is a **gauge-invariant** quantity: a global phase rotation `π → π + θ` shifts the source phase *and* the horizon phase equally, leaving `σ` unchanged. `σ` does not pick out an absolute phase zero; the horizon does, physically. The probe confirms (test 2): under a global rotation `θ = 0, 0.7, 2.5`, the source-horizon coherence, the double-slit visibility, and the MOND cross-term are all invariant. **Global phase stays unobservable; there is no global-U(1) breaking.**

## 3. Why local interference does not see it: co-local paths share the bias

A double-slit interferes two paths `A, B` of the *same* system at the *same* location. Write each phase as `π = π_loc + δ`, where `π_loc` is the location's horizon-biased common phase and `δ` is the path-specific (slit-geometry / gauge) phase. Because the horizon coupling is smooth on local scales, two co-local paths share the *same* `π_loc`, so the visibility
$$ V = |\langle e^{i(\pi_A - \pi_B)}\rangle| = |\langle e^{i(\delta_A - \delta_B)}\rangle| $$
depends only on the path difference `δ_A - δ_B`: **the horizon bias cancels.** The probe confirms (test 1): as `σ` runs from 0 to 0.89, `V` stays fixed at `0.9606`, identical to the no-bias value. The contrast case (paths given *independent* biases, i.e. not co-local) shows `V` collapsing, which proves the cancellation is load-bearing, not vacuous. And test 3 makes the scale-selectivity explicit: the co-local coherent cross-term is `~0` and `σ`-independent, while the local-horizon cross-term tracks `σ` exactly. **The `σ` effect is confined to the local-vs-cosmological relative phase, precisely where MOND lives, and is absent from all co-local (laboratory) interference.**

## 4. Gauge program: a global reference is not a local breaking

The gauge program (SU(N)×U(1), the photon) rests on *local* gauge invariance: local phase rotations are compensated by gauge fields. A horizon-imposed preferred *global* phase does not touch this, because global ≠ local: a global reference does not require a compensating gauge field and does not give the U(1) gauge boson (photon) a mass (that needs *local* symmetry breaking, a Higgs). So the electromagnetic sector's masslessness and the non-abelian gauge structure are untouched. The `σ`-reference is also consonant with ED's *existing* preferred-frame structure (the arrow P11, the horizon as a physical SCBU boundary): it is not a new ad hoc symmetry breaking beyond what ED already carries.

## 5. One assumption does both jobs

The consistency and the recast require the *same* physical input: **the horizon-phase bias is smooth on local scales, so co-local systems share it.** That single fact is (i) why co-local double-slit interference is bias-free (QM-safe, §3), and (ii) why the recast's local-local cross-term is `σ²`-suppressed rather than `σ` (Newton-safe). QM-safety and Newton-safety are not two separate assumptions; they are one. The alternative (a horizon bias varying rapidly on local scales) would *both* spoil the recast (local-local would not cancel) *and* threaten QM, so the natural picture is favored on both counts at once.
