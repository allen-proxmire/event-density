## 2. Dimensional: a scale-free action cannot give a₀ (decisive)

ED's derived action is `S = (1/16πG)∫ N√h [K_ij K^ij − λK² + ξ³R + η a²]` with `λ ∈ [0,1/3)`, `ξ=1`, `η→0`. Its couplings are **dimensionless** (`λ, ξ, η`), and the only dimensionful constant is `G`. MOND has a dimensionful acceleration scale `a₀ ~ 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s²`. There is no way to build a *fixed universal acceleration* from `G` and dimensionless couplings alone: any acceleration one forms (`GM/r²`) depends on the system's `M` and `r`. So **the standard khronometric action, being scale-free, cannot produce `a₀`, hence cannot produce MOND.** This is decisive on dimensional grounds before any dynamics.

## 3. Weak field: rescaled Newton, Keplerian curves, not MOND

The linearized Einstein-aether / khronometric weak field is a known result: for a static source it gives `Φ ~ −G_eff M/r` (with `G_eff = G/(1 − c₁₄/2)`-type rescaling) plus preferred-frame PPN corrections. The `r`-dependence is **unchanged** (still `1/r`), so the circular velocity `v² = G_eff M/r` falls as `1/√r` (Keplerian). The probe contrasts this with MOND (`v⁴ = G M a₀`, flat): the khronometric-linear curve drops from `~2000` to `~20` km/s over the galaxy, while MOND is flat at `~200` km/s. **The quadratic action does not flatten rotation curves.** MOND's flat curves require `Φ ~ +√(GMa₀) ln r` at large `r`, a different `r`-dependence the linear theory does not produce.

That nonlinear function *introduces* `a₀` (as the scale where `F` turns nonlinear) and gives the MOND `√` behaviour. But:

- `F(K)` is a **free function with a built-in `a₀`**: it is MOND inserted by hand, dressed in aether language, not derived.

- ED provides an `a₀` *intuition* (the horizon acceleration scale, `~c²/R_H`), but neither the nonlinear `F` nor a derivation of it.
