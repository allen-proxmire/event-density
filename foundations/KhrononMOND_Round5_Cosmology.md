**Foundations mapping round — opens the cosmological half of the unification (the regulator-family / clusters-CMB thread, which Round 4 showed is one thread). ## 1. The question, and what Round 4 already fixed

Round 4 established: the khronon's cosmological sector is the regulator family `𝒲(A², Θ)` evaluated near `(A → 0, Θ ∼ 1)`, sequestered from all static tests (the orthogonality theorem), and carrying three filters (BBN, Čerenkov, FRW stability). The standing debts of every MOND-class theory — **clusters and the CMB** — were flagged with an address: this sector. Round 5 asks the structural question:

> On FRW backgrounds, what *is* the Θ-sector, what would the cosmological data require of it, and does the class contain mechanisms that meet those requirements?

## 2. The khronon's FRW stress structure (form-level)

On FRW the aligned khronon is the comoving congruence: `a = 0`, `σ = 0`, `θ = 3H`. The active foliation terms and their cosmological roles:

1. **`λθ² ∼ λH²`** — renormalizes the Friedmann equation (an effective `G_{\rm cosmo} ≠ G_N`), the known khronometric effect, bounded by BBN. [I — class literature.] Inherited; nothing new.
2. **`𝒲(0, Θ)` — a dark fluid `ρ_𝒲(H)`.** A function of `Θ = θ/3H₀ = H/H₀` contributes to the Friedmann equation as a fluid whose density is a function of the expansion rate. Its two automatic features:
   - **Late times (`H → H₀`):** `ρ_𝒲 → const ∼ a₀²c²/G ∼ H₀²c²/G` — the Λ-order vacuum term of Round 4 §7.4 (form-tier; no value claimed).
   - **Early times (`H ≫ H₀`):** the behavior is the regulator member's choice — a pure `Θ²` growth folds into the `G_{\rm cosmo}` renormalization (BBN-bounded); other dependencies give other effective equations of state. **This is exactly where the family lives.**
3. **The perturbation sector** — `δ𝒲` fluctuations about FRW: the cosmologically *active* khronon mode. Its behavior — equation of state `w`, sound speed `c_s` — is what the CMB and structure formation actually probe. §4 is about this.

## 3. The requirements map (what the data demand of the sector)

For the Θ-sector to discharge the inherited debts, the cosmological data impose, qualitatively:

| Epoch / probe | Requirement on the Θ-sector |
|---|---|
| **BBN** | total `H²`-tracking contribution (the `λ`-term + any `Θ²` part of `𝒲`) inside the `G_{\rm cosmo}/G_N` bound |
| **Recombination / CMB peaks** | a component of the right abundance that **clusters like pressureless dust** (`w ≈ 0`, `c_s ≈ 0`) at recombination scales — the role particle dark matter plays in the standard fit |
| **Structure formation / clusters** | the same dust-like component supplying the missing cluster mass (the residual MOND alone cannot cover) |
| **Late times** | the Λ-order constant (automatic, §2.2) |
| **Čerenkov / stability** | propagating modes along cosmic-ray paths fast enough; perturbations ghost/gradient-stable on FRW |

The hard row is the second: MOND-class theories historically fail *there* — galactic dynamics they handle, but the CMB's acoustic-peak structure wants something that behaves like cold dust at `z ∼ 1100`.

## 4. The structural result: the soft spot is the doorway

Here is the round's central observation, and it is structural rather than model-dependent:

> **The condition for dust-like clustering is `c_s ≈ 0` — and a near-zero sound speed at small `A` is exactly the behavior Round 3 flagged as the deep-IR soft spot.** The feature that looked like the theory's vulnerability (the slowing, weakly-propagating near-vacuum scalar) is precisely the feature a dark-matter-like cosmological component *requires* (a fluid that does not free-stream, does not oscillate acoustically, and falls into potential wells like dust). **The degeneracy cure and the clusters/CMB debt are the same dial.**

Stated as the trade the regulator member must thread:

- **Too stiff** (`c_s ∼ 1` everywhere near vacuum): the degeneracy is over-cured, Čerenkov is trivially safe — but nothing clusters; the CMB debt stands in full.
- **Too soft** (`c_s = 0` exactly): maximal dust-like clustering (the CMB-friendly end) — but caustics form (the known pathology of pressureless scalar dust) and the strong-coupling concern returns.
- **The window** (small but nonzero `c_s` in the cosmological regime): dust-like clustering on CMB/cluster scales with caustics regulated — exactly the trade the *imperfect-fluid* refinements of the class navigate. [I — see §5.]

This converts Round 3's liability into the program's opportunity, and it sharpens what "choosing the regulator member" means: it is not bookkeeping — **it is the single choice that simultaneously sets the degeneracy cure, the Čerenkov compliance, and the theory's answer to clusters and the CMB.** One dial, three previously-separate items.

## 5. The class mechanisms (labeled I — directions, not verifications)

The khronometric class *contains* known constructions in which the preferred-foliation scalar supplies a cosmological dark-matter component:

1. **Mimetic dark matter** (Chamseddine–Mukhanov): a scalar constrained to unit timelike gradient — structurally a constrained khronon — contributes **exactly pressureless dust** to cosmology, with `c_s = 0`; its known issues (caustics; the need for a small sound speed) led to the *imperfect mimetic fluid* refinements. This is the `c_s → 0` end of §4's dial, realized in the literature.
2. **Dark matter as an integration constant** (Mukohyama, projectable Hořava): the global-only Hamiltonian constraint leaves a dust-like component whose **abundance is an initial condition**. Honesty: this mechanism lives in the *projectable* branch — a **neighboring** member of the Hořava family, not automatically ED's (the GR-II identification was the non-projectable/khronometric branch). Cited as the class's proof-of-concept that foliation gravity *can* manufacture dust, not as ED's mechanism.
3. **Khronometric/aether cosmology** generally (Blas–Lim and successors): the viable-corner analyses of the `λ`-sector on FRW. [I.]

**A consonance worth noting carefully.** In both class mechanisms the dark-matter *abundance* is not predicted — it enters as an initial condition / integration constant. Under ED's own methodology that is not a defect but a classification: **the existence of the dust channel would be form-level structure; the 5:1 abundance would be value-inherited** — exactly the form-FORCED / value-INHERITED split the corpus applies to constants. The class's "weakness" is ED's standing epistemic posture.

## 6. Honest tiering — what this round does and does not establish

**Established (structural):**
- The Θ-sector on FRW is a dark fluid `ρ_𝒲(H)` with an automatic Λ-order late-time constant and a family-dependent early-time behavior (§2).
- The dust-clustering requirement (`c_s ≈ 0`) coincides with the Round-3 near-vacuum behavior: **the soft spot and the clusters/CMB debt are one dial** (§4) — the round's load-bearing result.
- The requirements map (§3) and the three-way trade (clustering vs caustics vs Čerenkov) the regulator member must thread.
- The abundance-as-initial-condition feature of the class mechanisms is *consonant* with value-inheritance (§5).

**Not established (and said plainly):**
- No regulator member is chosen; no CMB spectrum is computed; no cluster profile is fit. **The clusters/CMB debt is shown to be *addressable within the sector ED already has* — not discharged.**
- ED's branch (non-projectable khronometric) is *not* shown to contain the dust mechanism automatically; the neighboring-branch result (§5.2) is a class proof-of-concept only.
- Whether one member can thread *all* of §4's window **and** the §3 table **and** Round 3's galactic-side filters simultaneously is the genuine open question — it is exactly the model-building task, now precisely specified.

## 7. Verdict and the arc's standing

**The clusters/CMB debt has moved from "inherited shortfall, address named" to "addressable through one dial, requirements specified."** The khronon's cosmological face is a dark fluid whose late-time behavior is automatically Λ-order, whose early-time behavior is the regulator family's single remaining freedom, and whose perturbation sector sits — by the same near-vacuum behavior Round 3 flagged — at the edge where dark-matter-like clustering lives. The class contains realized mechanisms at both ends of the dial. What remains is the model-building task in its sharpest form yet: **one function (`𝒲`'s Θ-dependence), five filters (BBN, dust-clustering, caustics, Čerenkov, FRW stability), and the data as judge.**
