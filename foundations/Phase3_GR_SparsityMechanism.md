## 1. The distinction I had been missing: T-violation ≠ Lorentz-violation

These are *different* symmetries, and conflating them is the error:

- **Time-asymmetry (T-violation):** the arrow — commitment is irreversible (P11). A *direction* of time. ED has this, full-strength, as its defining feature.
- **Lorentz-violation (preferred frame):** a preferred *rest frame* / a rigid global foliation. This is what `α₁, α₂` measure.

**T-violation does not imply Lorentz-violation.** The textbook proof: the Standard Model has CP-violation (hence T-violation, by CPT) and is *exactly Lorentz-invariant*. A theory can have a sharp arrow of time and no preferred frame at all. So the arrow being a full-strength primitive forces T-violation — but it does **not**, by itself, force the `O(1)` *Lorentz*-violation that the coarse-graining note assumed. That assumption was an extra step, not a consequence.

## 2. Why ED's preferred frame is largely *spontaneous*, not explicit

A *direction* of time (a timelike vector field `u^μ`) becomes a *foliation* (a preferred slicing, the khronon) only if it is hypersurface-orthogonal — i.e. only if there is a global time function whose level sets are synchronized "now" surfaces. Does ED's arrow supply that?

**V1 (finite-speed causation) says no, not rigidly.** Spacelike-separated regions are causally disconnected (single cone, P05; finite-width retarded kernel, V1). So their commitments are **causally independent** — nothing synchronizes "now" across spacelike separation at the fundamental level. There is no rigid, locally-enforced global simultaneity; the only thing that picks a global frame is the **cosmological** background — the actual matter distribution / the Hubble flow.

> **So ED's preferred frame is, at leading order, the *cosmological rest frame* — selected spontaneously by the matter content, exactly like the CMB rest frame.** And a spontaneously-selected cosmological frame in an otherwise Lorentz-invariant theory gives `α₁ = α₂ = 0` (GR in an FRW universe has a cosmic rest frame and *zero* preferred-frame PPN parameters). Explicit preferred-frame effects come only from *explicit* Lorentz violation in the action — and V1 spacelike-independence argues that ED's explicit part is **soft**.

This is the crux: the `α₁ = O(1)` expectation assumed ED's foliation is a *rigid, explicit* Lorentz-violating structure. ED's own causal structure (V1) argues it is mostly a *spontaneous* cosmological frame, which is Lorentz-invariant.

## 3. The three objections dissolve

The suppression hunt's objections all came from conflating distinct quantities:

1. *"Becoming is ongoing → dense commitments."* — Ongoing **T-violation** (the arrow always points forward) does not require dense **foliation-pinning** (global synchronization). The arrow is always on; the global "now" is still only loosely pinned (V1). Objection conflated T-violation with LV.
2. *"Gravity needs dense near-matter commitments."* — Gravity's strength is the **ratio** `κ/D = 8πG`, which is *rate-independent* (a steady-state field-equation relation). The commitment *density* that sets the foliation rigidity is a **separate** quantity. Strong gravity (a fixed ratio) is fully consistent with sparse foliation-pinning. Objection conflated the ratio with the density.
3. *"`λ` is a dimensionless ratio needing a hierarchy."* — The hierarchy is *supplied*: the metric stiffness `M_P²` comes from the substrate connectivity (P02, Planck-dense, always present); the foliation/khronon stiffness comes from the *foliation-pinning* events, whose effective density is set by V1-limited global synchronization, **not** the Planck density. `λ = (\text{soft foliation stiffness})/M_P² ≪ 1`. The hierarchy is the gap between *spontaneous-cosmological* (soft) and *substrate-geometric* (stiff).

None of the three survives once T-violation, the `κ/D` ratio, and the spontaneous/explicit split are kept distinct.
