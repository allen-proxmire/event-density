## 1. The question

The open piece was the *absolute* scale: is there a new free length constant hiding in the construction, or is the scale just the ℓ_P grain already postulated (P08)?

It multiplies reach, hence scales the emergent hop-distance overall. The test: does R₀ enter the *physics* (the metric exponent), or is it a pure unit that drops out?
