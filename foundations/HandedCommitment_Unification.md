## 1. The claim

Two facts the Standard Model puts in *by hand*:

- **Parity violation is maximal.** The weak force couples to a *pure* left-handed current (V−A). Not partial, not a tunable angle — maximal. The SM offers no reason.
- **The universe is matter, not antimatter.** A global asymmetry between matter and its mirror. The SM's CP violation is far too small to produce the observed amount; the origin is open (baryogenesis).

Both are *handedness* facts — one in the gauge sector (which spatial handedness the force sees), one in the cosmological sector (which of a matter/antimatter pair the universe kept). ED's claim:

> **They are the same choice.** ED's substrate commits irreversibly (P11), one-way in time (the arrow). If a single commitment carries a *handedness* — a screw-sense, correlating its time-advance with a phase-twist — then the universe's *first* commitment selects a screw-sense, and every subsequent commitment inherits it (first-arrival lock-in). That one global selection is **parity violation** seen in the fermion–gauge coupling and **matter dominance** seen cosmologically. One lock-in, two faces.

## 2. Why a single mechanism gives *both*, and why *both* are maximal

The supporting results from the #2b thread:

- **Handedness is a count, not a dial** (`SQ1d`, `SQ1f`). The net handedness from committed events is a *topological* quantity — the winding of the committed phase along the commitment sequence. A topological charge is quantized: it is full or nothing, never partial.
- **A locked sign gives full handedness, robust to noise** (`SQ1f`, `chiral_percommit.py`). If every commitment screws the same way (locked), the net winding accumulates (∼N) and survives P11 randomization (∼√N) — even at infinitesimal per-event bias. If the sign is unlocked, the net winding is zero (mirror-symmetric).

So the *same* structural fact — a globally locked screw-sense — produces a *maximal* (topological, all-or-nothing) handedness. Applied to the gauge sector that is **maximal parity violation**; applied to the cosmological sector that is a **complete** matter/antimatter selection. The maximality of *both* is explained by the *same* feature (topological lock-in), and it is the feature that distinguishes a lock-in from a tunable coupling — exactly why these two facts are maximal while ED's *non-topological* couplings (e.g. the preferred-frame α₁ ∼ ρ_event/ρ_Planck) are sparsity-suppressed to near zero (`SQ1d`). **Maximal where topological, tiny where not** — and that is the SM's actual pattern.

1. **Two open problems become one.** #2b's chiral-gauge gap and baryogenesis's lock-in are no longer separate: both reduce to *derive the first-arrival handed-commitment lock and its handedness-realization*. One derivation discharges both.

3. **A reason for maximality.** It supplies the missing *why* behind two facts the SM merely stipulates, from one ontological commitment (the arrow).

This is a hypothesis with two undischarged pieces — the *same* two that block baryogenesis and #2b individually:

Until both are discharged, this is a *coherent organizing hypothesis*, M-conjectural — the kind of structural claim that says "if ED is right, these are one thing," not "ED shows these are one thing."

## 5. What would confirm or refute it

- **Derive the lock** (closes piece 1): show first-arrival commitment dynamics select and globally propagate a screw-sense — a spontaneous breaking of the phase-screw direction — without postulating `P-BinaryAdmission`. This is the shared baryogenesis/#2b target.
- **Show the screw is chiral, not vector** (closes piece 2): the decisive question is whether ED's P09 phase couples to the emergent fermion *chirally* (γ⁵, like the weak force) or *vectorially* (like electromagnetism). A vector coupling kills the unification; a chiral coupling confirms the gauge face. The structural reason for cautious optimism: the P09 phase is transported (P05) along the *time-oriented* arrow, so it is tied to γ⁰ — and γ⁵ = iγ⁰γ¹γ²γ³ is built from precisely that γ⁰, unlike electromagnetism's direction-blind vector coupling. (See the next foundations step.)
- **A correlation prediction (if true):** the gauge handedness and the cosmological matter-choice are not independent — they are the *same* sign. Whether that yields anything observable (a relation between the parity-violation handedness and the baryon-asymmetry sign) is itself open, and likely not testable with current data, but it is the kind of non-trivial tie a genuine unification should carry.
