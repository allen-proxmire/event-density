## The three structural blockages

**FSC-1.1 (substrate-level, structural impossibility).** The composition P09 + P05 + P11 does not force quantized substrate loop holonomy. The blockage is over-determined by three primitives: P09 specifies polarity in the continuous group $U(1) \cong S^1$ (dominant); P11's uniform-$U(1)$ randomization is rotation-invariant and erases (rather than quantizes) accumulated phase; P11 events are chain-attached, not graph-attached, so the Dirac-monopole-analog linking relation has no substrate realization. Substrate loop holonomy $H(\gamma)$ is *continuous* in $U(1)$; the topological-winding route to $\alpha$-as-substrate-integer is structurally impossible.

**FSC-2.1 (substrate-level, structural underdetermination).** The cross-overlap integral $I(\delta) = \int V_1(x) V_1(x+\delta)\, d^4x$ is dimensionless, well-defined under N1 + T18 + Stone-normalization, and finite. But its value is FORCED-FORM-INHERITED-VALUE: the integral is the substrate-regulated form of a one-loop QFT vacuum-polarization diagram, and its regulated value is regulator-shape-dependent. The 13-primitive set fixes substrate macroscopic structure ($D = 3+1$ + homogeneity + scale $\ell_P$) but does not pin substrate microscopic adjacency / connectivity, which determines V1's spectral shape.

**FSC-3.5.1 (emergent-level, no marginal fixed points).** One-loop Wilsonian RG β-functions for the marginal sector $(\lambda, 1/e^2, e)$ have universal (shape-independent) leading-log coefficients but exhibit no IR-attractive fixed points. The shape-independent ratio $\rho_1 = \lambda/e^2$ has flow with discriminant $-1799/9 < 0$ (no real fixed point); monotonic Landau-pole-class running. Standard scalar-QED inheritance.

## The positive structural finding (Findings FSC-3.6.1 / 3.6.2)

Extending the marginal-sector flow to include the Tier-5 ratio operators (P04 four-band allocation $\{r_i, r_a, r_e, r_c\}$ and reversible/irreversible ratio) produces a fundamentally different result:

> The transition-rate matrix $\Gamma^{(\mathrm{LL})}_{\alpha\beta}$ governing inter-band bandwidth flow under substrate-RG coarse-graining is a Markov-generator-class matrix (column sums vanish, off-diagonals non-negative). All six pairs of bands $\{i \leftrightarrow a, a \leftrightarrow e, i \leftrightarrow e, c \leftrightarrow i, c \leftrightarrow a, c \leftrightarrow e\}$ are connected by closed-arc substrate kernels (V1, V5, P11 statistics), so $\Gamma^{(\mathrm{LL})}$ is irreducible. Perron–Frobenius applied to the matrix exponential $e^{\Gamma t}$ guarantees a unique IR-attractive shape-independent stationary distribution $\{r_\alpha^{(*)}\}$ — a substrate-RG universal.

The pattern: **absolute coupling magnitudes are non-universal; band-allocation and reversible/irreversible ratios are universal**. This matches the standard-QFT pattern (critical exponents universal, coupling magnitudes not) and extends Arc RG's 0.6-ratio precedent to the full P04 sector.
