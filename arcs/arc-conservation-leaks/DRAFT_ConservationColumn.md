## Preamble — what this does NOT claim

1. **No new physics.** Every conservation fact below is standard: local covariant conservation ($\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$) always holds; a globally conserved energy generally does **not** exist in a dynamic spacetime. This is textbook general relativity. ED reproduces it.
2. **The lens is a reading, not a theorem** — exactly as the parent ledger states of itself. This note adds one structural row to that ledger (the conservation column) plus a substrate-native mechanism for it, both conditional on the thirteen primitives (Paper_087).
3. **No novel prediction.** The one place ED *could* depart from GR here — a substrate-level total that stays conserved while the emergent energy leaks — is named as an open question (§8), not claimed.
4. **Reproduces, does not overturn.** Inside every regime where a conservation law is tested, ED predicts no deviation.

## 1. The gap the parent ledger leaves open

The coarse-graining ledger reads every emergent symmetry as the *receipt for a discard*, via one mechanism: **order-erasure.** Averaging is permutation-blind, so a blur forgets which commitment came first, and that is where reversibility, unitarity, frame-democracy, and diffeomorphism-invariance come from. Its recipe table (§14 there) is complete for those symmetries.

But that table has **no conservation law in it** — no energy, no momentum. This is not an omission; it is a boundary. Conservation laws are Noether charges of **continuous translation symmetries**, and those do not come from forgetting *order*. They come from a different discard: assuming the backdrop is **uniform**.

## 2. Two discards, two classes of symmetry

The ledger's symmetries and the Noether conservation laws sit on orthogonal axes:

- **Order-erasure** (the parent ledger): the blur forgets the *sequence* of events → time-reversal symmetry, unitarity, frame-democracy. About the **order** of what happens.
- **Backdrop-uniformity** (this column): the blur treats the arena as *the same everywhere and everywhen* → the Noether conservation laws (energy ← time-translation invariance; momentum ← space-translation invariance). About the **uniformity** of where and when it happens.

A theory can keep one and discard the other. That is why conservation needs its own row.

## 3. The conservation row

| Kept | Assumed / discarded | Artifact-symmetry | Where it leaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| the smooth conserved density (energy, momentum) | the **exact uniformity of the backdrop** (translation invariance) | the conservation **law** | wherever the backdrop is not uniform — a **dynamic** spacetime — with leak size set by how much the backdrop changes across the patch/interval |

The conservation law is what you get by *assuming* a perfectly uniform arena. Where the assumption is exact, the law is exact. Where the arena actually changes, the conserved quantity **leaks**, and the leak is a **gradient, not a cliff** — its magnitude scales with the backdrop's rate of change over the region measured.

## 4. The substrate mechanism — already in the primitives (P13 + P11)

The mechanism does not need a new postulate. It is latent in two existing primitives, which the primitive paper (Paper_087) already holds apart:

- **P13 (time homogeneity)** is defined as a symmetry of the **law**: *"the substrate-level laws at time $t$ are identical to those at $t'$; $\partial_t$ is a substrate-level symmetry."* By Noether this yields a **local conserved current** — energy is the charge of time-translation. **Local conservation is exact.** (A symmetric *law* gives a local current; a globally conserved *charge* would additionally require a stationary *state* — a timelike Killing symmetry — which P13 does not assert.)
- **P11 (commitment-irreversibility, the arrow)** makes the *state* non-stationary: *"the collapse is irreversible; no substrate-level operation maps post-commitment to pre-commitment state."* The record only accumulates. So the substrate is **never globally stationary**, and there is **no global time-translation symmetry** of the actual history → **no globally conserved energy.**

Crucially, the corpus already couples them exactly this way: P13's own definition exists to make **P11 "the only temporal-asymmetry primitive."** So the split — *symmetric law, directional state* — is the corpus's, not this note's. Put together:

> **Local conservation is exact (P13). Global conservation leaks (P11). The leak is the arrow resurfacing in the energy books.**

This is precisely the parent ledger's §15 signature — *"a discarded column never fully vanishes; it leaks"* — now applied to conservation, with the arrow (the substrate's one genuine irreversibility) as the leak's source.

**F4 is not tripped.** Paper_006_5's falsifier F4 refutes energy non-conservation *from time-translation symmetry* — i.e., where the symmetry **holds**. The leak occurs precisely where the symmetry is **absent** (a changing backdrop), so Noether yields no charge and non-conservation is expected, not a refutation. Local (F4-protected) and global (leaking) coexist.

## 5. It reproduces GR — and localizes energy more cleanly

This is exactly GR's structure: covariant $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$ holds locally and always; a global conserved energy generally fails in a dynamic spacetime. **ED reproduces GR here — no departure.** Its added value is the substrate reading of the localization:

- The dynamics arc builds a substrate Noether stress-energy $T^{\mu\nu}_{\text{sub}}$ from P03 + P13 translation-invariance, uses it as a **local flux**, and coarse-grains (DCGT) to the standard radiation and Friedmann laws — never defining a global energy (`Paper_ED_GW_00`, `Paper_ED_Dyn_02`, `Paper_ED_Cos_05`). *(Honest boundary: `Dyn_02` is M2 — the substrate-graph derivation of the stress-energy* form *per regime is INHERITED via QFT-analog, not substrate-derived, Q1/Q2 open. - `Paper_ED_GW_00` goes further: gravitational-wave energy is a **local ED-gradient load**, and GR's gauge-dependent **stress-energy pseudotensor is explicitly *replaced*.** The pseudotensor is GR's failed attempt at a *global* gravitational energy; ED discards it for a clean local quantity requiring no gauge fixing. ED does not merely tolerate the local-only picture — it commits to it.

## 6. The leak's profile — largest early, off at the uniform end

Because the leak tracks backdrop non-uniformity, it has a cosmological profile, and the corpus supplies both ends:

- **Dynamic / structured epoch:** the backdrop changes fastest → the leak is largest. (The redshifting CMB is this: photon energy is not globally conserved, and on this reading the expanding backdrop is where it goes.)
- **Uniform end (`Paper_ED_Cos_05`):** dark energy is the late-universe **saturation regime**, where *"expansion-driven dilution drives the substrate toward uniform-Ψ"* → de Sitter. As the backdrop smooths to uniform, time-translation symmetry is **restored**, and with it a (featureless, de Sitter) conserved energy. **The leak shuts off as the universe empties out.**

So conservation is not simply "broken" — it fades in and out with the arena's uniformity: absent where the universe is busy becoming, restored where it has finished. This grounds the CCC-style intuition that the ultimate uniformity *restores* symmetry, in an existing ED result.
