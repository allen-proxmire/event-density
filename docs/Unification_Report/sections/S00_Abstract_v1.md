## Abstract

Event Density (ED) is a substrate ontology whose unifying power comes from one primitive, the **arrow of time** — the irreversible act of commitment — doing load-bearing work across sectors that mainstream physics treats as separate and even as in tension. The same arrow selects the quantum pointer basis (einselection) and *is* gravity's preferred time (the khronon), so quantum measurement's "which basis is definite" and gravity's "which time is flowing" are one primitive wearing two hats, and the problem of time dissolves rather than being reconciled. On that spine, ED is **form-complete across every box a unified framework must check** and **value-inherited exactly where the Standard Model's free parameters live** — a structural consequence of a pure-relation ontology having no intrinsic scalar, not a gap, and no worse than the Standard Model's own standing on those numbers.

The report states, box by box and at explicit honesty tiers, what is earned. Quantum mechanics is **reconstructed** from commitment by mapping onto Piron–Solèr (orthogonality reduced to operational distinguishability, the complex field selected, Born non-contextuality grounded; the residual is the Solèr lattice rigor of an inherited theorem). Gravity is **closed** — the weak-field Einstein metric, the khronometric class with `c_T = c`, `α₂ = 0` exactly, and the same khronon field giving dark matter (MOND deep field) and dark energy (one Λ), with a new healthy-branch bound `λ < 1/3, λ ≠ 1` placing ED off general relativity. The gauge sector's **shape is derived** — `SU(N)` form, non-abelian gauging, the `F²` action, the mass-gap mechanism, and a single hypercharge (the arrow wearing a phase) — the structure the Standard Model postulates wholesale. Charge is **native** (quantized winding, an integral Gauss law), with the smooth electromagnetic field a coarse-grained shadow. Chirality is a **theorem**: the parity-clean substrate is vector for every channel-count, so parity violation is necessarily spontaneous, while *which* force is chiral is inherited representation theory. And the matter sector delivers a **native binding-mass mechanism** — a lone excitation is massless, mass comes from binding, "mass without mass," the dominant form of real mass — cleanly separated from time dilation.

The report is as loud about its limits as its results. Two structural questions are genuinely open — the gauge multiplicities `{1,2,3}` and whether exact charge conservation forces anomaly-freedom on the emergent chiral content — and they reduce to one deep arc (the substrate-to-Dirac reduction) plus one delegatable computation (a linkless-embedding test of the internal dimension), both unsolved in the Standard Model too. One wall is *proven*: the finite-memory substrate cannot reach the primality escape layer, theorem-anchored. The constants are inherited by design. And, decisively, **no distinctive, argument-ending prediction is confirmed yet**: ED makes one confirmed forward prediction and survives the tests that killed rival gravity theories, but the bets that would single it out — a universal decoherence-envelope ratio, a step-like merging-cluster knee, an architecture-independent mass wall, an exactly-zero baryonic Tully–Fisher scatter — are all still open.

The claim, stated at its honest ceiling, is this: one primitive, the arrow of time, recurs as the mechanism across quantum mechanics, gravity, and the Standard Model's structure, most strikingly where quantum mechanics and gravity were supposed to be irreconcilable. That recurrence is a serious case for a unified framework. It is not yet the empirical case that would end the argument, and this document carries both openly — which is what makes it verification-grade rather than dismissible.
