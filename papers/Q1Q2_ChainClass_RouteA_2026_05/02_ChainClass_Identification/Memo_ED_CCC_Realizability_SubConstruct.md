## §4 Elimination of alternative realizability routes

### §4.1 Alternative R1 — Stochastic homogenization

**Mechanism:** pre-inflationary substrate dynamics smooths arbitrary initial $\Psi$-distributions to uniform $\Psi$ through stochastic / thermalization-like behavior.

**Examination:** stochastic homogenization requires substrate-graph dynamics that *operate before kernel reactivation*. Per Paper_087 P12 + Paper_072, sub-threshold substrate has V1 + V5 + individuation deactivated — no kernel-active dynamics to drive stochastic evolution. Above-threshold dynamics could in principle smooth fluctuations, but at the threshold-crossing instant the substrate is already in the sub-threshold uniform state (§3.1) — no need for post-threshold smoothing.

**Outcome:** **R1 not load-bearing — sub-threshold uniformity (§3.1) supplies the needed uniform-$\Psi$ without requiring post-threshold stochastic homogenization.** R1 is consistent with §3.1's mechanism but does not supply additional content; it is vocabulary-level relative to the sub-threshold uniformity argument.

### §4.2 Alternative R2 — Kernel-balanced configuration

**Mechanism:** uniform-$\Psi$ realized via specific V1/V5 kernel-balance configurations that satisfy $\nabla_{V_1}\Psi = -\nabla_{V_5}\Psi$ at every locus.

**Examination:** per C3 audit Q-C3-2b, kernel-balanced configurations are a measure-zero exception in configuration space — they could supply vacuum-energy continuum from *non-uniform* substrate, not from uniform substrate. **R2 addresses a different question than Q-C3-3** (R2 is about converse-direction non-uniqueness; Q-C3-3 is about forward-direction realizability).

**Outcome:** **R2 inapplicable to Q-C3-3.** Kernel-balanced configurations are not a realizability route for uniform $\Psi$; they are a measure-zero converse-direction exception class.

### §4.3 Alternative R3 — Single-aeon initial-condition route

**Mechanism:** ED admits non-cyclic single-aeon cosmology (CCC row 17 negative branch); uniform $\Psi$ at inflation is the structural-initial-condition assumption of the single cosmological cycle, not derived from a prior-aeon ignition.

**Examination:** under single-aeon framing, the substrate-side initial conditions of the universe are *posited as initial conditions* without substrate-graph mechanism derivation. **This is the cyclic-row-17-OPEN negative branch.** R3 is the alternative framing in which Cos_01's saturation realizability is supplied at IC level *without* a prior-aeon ignition mechanism.

**Outcome:** **R3 is admissible as the CCC row 17 negative branch.** Under R3, Q-C3-3 closes at IC-INHERITED level with no cyclic-dependency at all — uniform $\Psi$ at inflation is an initial-condition assumption that does not require CCC's cyclic framework. **R3 supplies Cos_01 M3 upgrade without inheriting CCC row 17 OPEN status.**

**Mechanism:** $\Psi$ remains uniform indefinitely in a quiescent saturation state with V1 + V5 deactivated; some external substrate-graph event (boundary collision; primitive-set-internal transition) triggers V1 + V5 activation and inflation begins.
