## Abstract

The Event Density (ED) Forcing Papers (#1–#19) establish that, *given* a small set of substrate primitives, every load-bearing structure of standard QM, QFT, gauge theory, the Hamiltonian / mass framework, gravity, and the kernel-level arrow of time is forced — admitting no consistent alternative on a substrate satisfying the named primitives. This leaves an unforced root: *why those primitives?* This meta-paper opens the program-level investigation of upstream forcing for the load-bearing substrate primitives. The full dependency-graph audit (Appendix A) identifies ~16 load-bearing items grouped into three centrality tiers: **HIGH** (P04 core, P09 $U(1)$ polarity, P04 §1.5 four-band partition), **MED** (P11, P03, P07, V1), and **LOW** (12 items including P06, P13, V5, HYD, P10, HOL, DEC, P12). Five candidate forcing routes are identified: **symmetry-minimality (A)**, **operational adequacy (B)**, **compositional closure (C)**, **information-theoretic constraints (D)**, and **category-theoretic necessity (E)**. Section 5.2 establishes the forcing priority order: M-1 = P09 (highest impact on the displaced-postulate critique), M-2 = P04 core, M-3 = P04 §1.5, then Tiers 2–5. Each primitive is tagged: **likely forceable** (~7), **mixed** (~4), **likely residue** (~5). The meta-paper's role is to convert the QM-reconstruction face of ED from "substrate-vocabulary variant of operational reconstruction" to "substrate ontology with explicitly named structural commitments at the deepest level." Some primitives will be fully forced under Routes A–E; others will reduce to honest residue categories. Either outcome eliminates the displaced-postulate critique in its strongest form.

### 6.1 P09 ($U(1)$-valued polarity) — Tag: **LIKELY FORCEABLE** — Priority: M-1

**Sketch.** Polarity must be a continuous angular primitive (rules out $\mathbb{Z}_n$ — discrete angular content fails to support continuous-phase interference observed empirically). It must be compact (rules out $\mathbb{R}$ — non-compact angular content fails Frobenius classification needed for $\mathbb{C}$ rather than $\mathbb{H}$ as the substrate division algebra). The unique compact continuous abelian Lie group is $U(1)$. Coecke-Kissinger framework supplies the category-theoretic version: $U(1)$ is the unique continuous-classical structure on a dagger-symmetric-monoidal category whose self-dual basis matches the substrate-level interference content.

**Residue risk.** Low. Worst case: "$U(1)$ polarity is minimum operational adequacy for substrate to recover continuous-phase interference, which is empirically observed."
