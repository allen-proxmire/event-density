## 1. Framing

### 1.1 What the four-band partition is in the ED substrate

The Event Density (ED) substrate's primitive structural commitment to bandwidth — Primitive P04 — has two components that the program has historically bundled together. The first, **P04 core**, says that each channel $K$ carries a non-negative real-valued bandwidth $b_K \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ that is *additive* across disjoint channel decompositions: if $K = K_1 \sqcup K_2$, then $b_K = b_{K_1} + b_{K_2}$. The second, **P04 §1.5**, says that this bandwidth, on each channel and at each substrate locus, further decomposes into four mutually orthogonal structural bands:

$$b_K(u) = b_K^{\mathrm{int}}(u) + b_K^{\mathrm{adj}}(u) + b_K^{\mathrm{env}}(u) + b_K^{\mathrm{com}}(u),$$

with each band carrying a distinct operational role:

- **Internal** ($b^{\mathrm{int}}$): bandwidth allocated to sustaining the chain's own rule-type identity. The "what-it-is" content of the chain.
- **Adjacency** ($b^{\mathrm{adj}}$): bandwidth allocated to coupling with the immediate participation-adjacent neighborhood. The "how-it-moves" content (kinematic).
- **Environmental** ($b^{\mathrm{env}}$): bandwidth allocated to coupling with the broader environmental bath. The "how-it-decoheres" content.
- **Commitment-reserve** ($b^{\mathrm{com}}$): bandwidth allocated toward commitment events. The "how-it-measures" content.

The four-band partition is a *primitive* substrate-level structural commitment. It is not derived from chain dynamics, not derived from kernel content, not derived from anything in Forcing Papers #1–#19. It is the substrate's intrinsic operational organization at the bandwidth level.

### 1.2 Why two conjugate pairs, not four independent bands

The four bands are not four arbitrarily-chosen orthogonal directions; they organize into **two conjugate pairs**. Each pair represents a substrate-level structural duality:

- **Kinematic conjugacy: Internal ↔ Adjacency.** The chain's relation to its own identity versus its relation to its immediate neighborhood. A chain entirely committed to internal bandwidth has no kinematic coupling — it is structurally inert; a chain entirely committed to adjacency bandwidth has no rule-type identity — it dissolves into its neighborhood. Operational physics requires both, in conjugate balance.
- **Dissipative conjugacy: Environmental ↔ Commitment-reserve.** The chain's relation to bath dissipation versus its relation to measurement-event allocation. A chain entirely committed to environmental bandwidth decoheres without ever committing; a chain entirely committed to commitment-reserve bandwidth commits prematurely without coupling to a bath. Operational physics requires both, in conjugate balance.

The conjugate-pair structure is not decorative. It is what makes the substrate capable of supporting both *kinematic content* (chain dynamics in spacetime) and *information processing content* (decoherence + commitment as the substrate-level mechanism for measurement outcomes). Without two distinct conjugate pairs, the substrate would conflate kinematic and information-processing operational categories.

The grouping is a $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ structure: the first $\mathbb{Z}_2$ distinguishes "is" (internal/environmental) from "does" (adjacency/commitment-reserve); the second $\mathbb{Z}_2$ distinguishes "kinematic" (internal/adjacency) from "dissipative" (environmental/commitment-reserve). The four-band partition realizes this $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ symmetry-minimal structure.

## 6. Alternative Encodings $\{A1-A6\}$ and $\{B1-B6\}$

Per the forcing-paper genre, this section enumerates structural alternatives to the four-band partition. The exclusion arguments for each appear in §8.

### 6.A. A-series — Structural alternatives within the substrate-ontology framework

**A1. Two-band partition $\{b^\mathrm{self}, b^\mathrm{other}\}$.**
Two bands distinguishing only what is "internal to the chain" versus what couples to "everything else." Would conflate kinematic motion with environmental decoherence and conflate measurement allocation with rule-identity.

**A2. Three-band partition $\{b^\mathrm{int}, b^\mathrm{adj}, b^\mathrm{ext}\}$.**
Three bands distinguishing internal, kinematic-adjacent, and "everything-external" (combining environmental + commitment-reserve). Conflates decoherence with measurement.

**A3. Five-or-more-band partition.**
Adding a fifth band to ED's four. Candidates: a "potential-coupling" band beyond adjacency; a "memory" band beyond commitment-reserve; a meta-channel band. Each would have to correspond to a structurally distinct operational role.

**A4. Asymmetric partition.**
A four-element partition where the bands do not organize into two conjugate pairs but instead form an asymmetric structure (e.g., one "primary" band and three "secondary" bands; or four bands with no duality structure).

**A5. Non-conjugate partition.**
A four-element partition where the bands are mutually orthogonal but lack the $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ structure — e.g., four bands organized as a 4-cycle, a tetrahedral structure, or four arbitrary independent operational categories.

**A6. Partition without bandwidth additivity.**
A four-element partition that is structurally similar to P04 §1.5 but with bandwidth non-additive across the bands (e.g., multiplicative composition, max-composition, or nonlinear composition rule).

### 6.B. B-series — Mainstream alternatives from physics frameworks

**B1. Classical phase-space partitions.**
Hamiltonian-mechanics-style partition of phase space into position-coordinate slabs and momentum-coordinate slabs. Phase-space measures (Liouville, microcanonical, canonical) implement this partition. Bandwidth in this case would be probability-density mass on phase-space regions.

**B2. Real-Hilbert-space partitions.**
Partitions of the state space of real quantum mechanics (RQM) into orthogonal subspaces, with bandwidth as squared-projection norm. Does not require complex phase content.

**B3. Quaternionic partitions.**
Partitions of quaternionic-QM state space, with bandwidth as quaternionic-norm content.

**B4. POVM-based partitions.**
Partitions defined by positive operator-valued measures: $\{E_1, E_2, \ldots, E_n\}$ with $\sum E_i = \mathbb{1}$. Bandwidth in this case is the expectation value of each POVM effect.

**B5. Stochastic-process partitions.**
Partitions arising in stochastic mechanics (Nelson stochastic mechanics, GRW collapse models, CSL): bandwidth as probability mass over diffusion-trajectory bins.

**B6. Field-operator partitions.**
QFT-level partitions: bandwidth content allocated across mode operators $\{a_k^\dagger a_k\}$ of a quantum field, with band-structure determined by mode-occupation-number.

The A-series alternatives are *structurally within the substrate-ontology framework* — they assume a substrate-level bandwidth partition but differ in count, organization, or composition rules. The B-series alternatives operate from outside the substrate-ontology framework, in standard or alternative QM/QFT frameworks. Both must be excluded for the forcing argument to close.

## 7. Constructive Necessity

This section derives the four-band partition from $\{C^*\}$ + M-1 + background mathematics, without invoking any Forcing Paper #1–#19 result. The derivation proceeds in five steps.

### 7.1 Operational categories require at least four bands (Route C)

Under $\{C^*\}$ C*5, the substrate must support at minimum four operational categories: rule-identity, kinematic-coupling, decoherence-coupling, and measurement-event-content. These are forced by the structural-normative goal C*4 — each operational category is required for the substrate to recover one or more empirically observed phenomena:

- **Rule-identity** is needed for the substrate to support persistent chain structure (without it, chains would dissolve into their environments).
- **Kinematic-coupling** is needed for the substrate to support chain motion in spacetime (without it, chains would be structurally inert).
- **Decoherence-coupling** is needed for the substrate to support loss of phase coherence (empirically observed in all measurement-context interactions; without it, the substrate would predict pure coherent superpositions persisting indefinitely).
- **Measurement-event-content** is needed for the substrate to support discrete observed outcomes (without it, the substrate would predict only continuous wave-like phenomena).

By **Route C (compositional closure)**, distinct operational categories must correspond to distinct bands. A partition with fewer than four bands would conflate at least two of these operational categories, producing a substrate that cannot operationally distinguish them. Such a substrate fails C*4 — it cannot recover the empirical distinction between (say) kinematic motion and decoherence, or between decoherence and measurement.

**Conclusion of step 7.1.** The substrate's bandwidth partition has *at least* four bands.

### 7.2 Operational categories require at most four bands (Route C)

The same Route C compositional-closure argument also constrains the maximum partition size. A fifth band would have to correspond to a structurally distinct fifth operational category — distinct from rule-identity, kinematic-coupling, decoherence-coupling, and measurement-event-content.

Candidate fifth categories considered:

- **"Potential-energy band"** (chain's bandwidth coupling to static potentials). Structural analysis: potential-energy content is *not* a distinct operational category at the substrate level; it manifests as a modification of the adjacency-band's bandwidth-flow under external influence. The substrate-level potential-energy is downstream content (Paper #6's $V(\hat{x})$ derivation), not a primitive operational category.
- **"Memory band"** (chain's bandwidth allocated to V5 cross-chain memory content). Structural analysis: memory content is *not* a distinct operational category at the substrate level either; V5 cross-chain correlations are downstream kernel-level content acting *within* the existing four-band partition (specifically, modulating environmental → commitment-reserve coupling).
- **"Meta-channel band"** (chain's bandwidth allocated to inter-channel coupling). Structural analysis: inter-channel coupling at the substrate level is *channel composition*, not a separate operational category. Channel composition is implemented by P03 + P07 (channel structure) at the locus level, not by a separate bandwidth band.
- **"Quantum-error-correction band"** (chain's bandwidth for QEC content). Structural analysis: QEC is downstream content of the Q-COMPUTE arc, operating within the four-band partition; not a primitive operational category.

None of these candidates corresponds to a structurally distinct operational category at the primitive substrate level. Any fifth band would either split one of the existing four (artificial split, violating Route C compositional closure) or duplicate an existing category (redundant, also violating C*7).

**Conclusion of step 7.2.** The substrate's bandwidth partition has *exactly* four bands.

### 7.3 The four bands organize into two conjugate pairs (Route A symmetry-minimality)

The four operational categories of step 7.1 (rule-identity, kinematic-coupling, decoherence-coupling, measurement-event-content) are not four arbitrary categories. They exhibit two structural dualities:

- **"Is/does" duality.** Rule-identity and decoherence-coupling are *state-like* — they describe what the chain *is* (rule-identity) and what bath it loses coherence into (decoherence-coupling). Kinematic-coupling and measurement-event-content are *action-like* — they describe what the chain *does* (kinematic motion) and what discrete events it commits to (measurement-events). The "is/does" distinction is a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ structural duality.

- **"Kinematic/dissipative" duality.** Rule-identity and kinematic-coupling concern the chain's spatiotemporal organization (its identity + how it moves through spacetime). Decoherence-coupling and measurement-event-content concern the chain's information-processing organization (how it loses coherence + how it produces outcomes). The "kinematic/dissipative" distinction is a $\mathbb{Z}_2$ structural duality.

These two dualities are *independent* — neither is derivable from the other. Together they form a $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ symmetry structure acting on the four-band partition:

$$\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2 = \langle \tau, \sigma \mid \tau^2 = \sigma^2 = (\tau\sigma)^2 = e \rangle$$

with $\tau$ the "is ↔ does" involution and $\sigma$ the "kinematic ↔ dissipative" involution. The four bands form the unique faithful $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ action on bandwidth content:

| | Kinematic | Dissipative |
|---|---|---|
| **Is** | Internal | Environmental |
| **Does** | Adjacency | Commitment-reserve |

By **Route A symmetry-minimality**, the substrate's bandwidth partition must realize the smallest faithful action of this symmetry. Four bands realize this minimum; any decoration (e.g., adding a "central" band fixed by both involutions) would violate compositional closure (Route C) since no fifth operationally-distinct category supports it.

**Conclusion of step 7.3.** The four bands organize into two conjugate pairs: kinematic conjugacy (internal ↔ adjacency) and dissipative conjugacy (environmental ↔ commitment-reserve).

### 7.4 Bandwidth additivity across the partition (Route D)

The substrate's bandwidth on each channel is the substrate-level non-negative real-valued primitive (per $\{C^*\}$ C*2). Under **Route D (information-theoretic constraints)**, the partition of bandwidth across the four bands must satisfy Shannon-Khinchin entropy axioms applied to the bandwidth-fraction distribution $\{b_K^\alpha / b_K \mid \alpha \in \{\mathrm{int, adj, env, com}\}\}$.

Specifically, **Shannon-Khinchin axiom 4** (additivity of entropy under independent events) requires that bandwidth in disjoint substrate-level structural categories sums. For the four-band partition:

$$b_K = b_K^\mathrm{int} + b_K^\mathrm{adj} + b_K^\mathrm{env} + b_K^\mathrm{com}.$$

Non-additive composition rules (multiplicative, max-combinatorial, nonlinear) violate axiom 4 and fail Shannon-Khinchin information-theoretic consistency.

This step jointly forces P04-core's bandwidth additivity in the context of the four-band partition. (The dedicated independent forcing of P04-core in its full generality — including for *arbitrary* partitions, not just the four-band — is the subject of a future M-paper or absorbed into a revised M-2 extension.)

**Conclusion of step 7.4.** Bandwidth is additive across the four-band partition.

## 9. Falsifiers

The forcing argument is empirically meaningful — it could be falsified by empirical or structural observations refuting any of its key claims.

### 9.F1 Operationally distinct fifth category

Discovery of a substrate-level operational category structurally distinct from rule-identity, kinematic-coupling, decoherence-coupling, and measurement-event-content would falsify step 7.2 (and hence the four-band maximum). Candidate: a *purely informational* operational category not subsumed by decoherence or measurement (e.g., a substrate-level quantum-erasure mechanism not equivalent to environmental coupling or commitment-allocation). *Honest assessment:* no empirically known category fails the subsumption check. Candidate disconfirmation: continued failure to identify a fifth category strengthens the forcing.

### 9.F2 Conflated operational categories at the substrate level

Discovery that two of the four operational categories (e.g., decoherence and measurement) are structurally *identical* at the primitive substrate level would falsify step 7.1 (forcing fewer than four bands). *Honest assessment:* the operational distinction between decoherence and measurement is structurally robust — decoherence is bath-induced, measurement is commitment-induced, and they have different empirical signatures (decoherence preserves quantum state in a wider basis; measurement produces definite outcomes).

### 9.F3 Non-$\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ duality structure

Discovery that the substrate's two dualities (is/does, kinematic/dissipative) are non-independent (one derivable from the other) would falsify step 7.3 (forcing a different symmetry group than $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$). *Honest assessment:* the two dualities are operationally independent — the is/does distinction is about state-vs-process; the kinematic/dissipative distinction is about spatiotemporal-vs-informational. Neither implies the other.

### 9.F4 Non-additive bandwidth empirically observed

Discovery of substrate-level bandwidth content that fails Shannon-Khinchin axiom 4 — e.g., bandwidth interacting non-additively across "independent" channel decompositions — would falsify step 7.4. *Honest assessment:* the additivity of bandwidth is a structural commitment closely tied to the additivity of probability ($\sigma$-additivity, empirically robust). Falsifying axiom-4-additivity at the substrate level would entail empirical failure of $\sigma$-additivity downstream — a deeply consequential observation not currently in evidence.

### 9.F5 Empirical refutation of M-1 closure

Discovery that M-1's $U(1)$ forcing fails (e.g., empirical observation of a fundamentally $\mathbb{Z}_n$-valued substrate angular content) would propagate to falsifying the present paper's exclusion of B2 (real-Hilbert-space) and B3 (quaternionic) partitions. *Honest assessment:* M-1's forcing is over-determined (Routes A + E + B converge); falsifying it requires structural-level disconfirmation of multiple independent forcing routes.

- **$\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$.** The Klein four-group, the direct product of two cyclic groups of order 2. Has four elements; acts faithfully on a four-element set via the natural left-multiplication action. The smallest non-cyclic abelian group of order 4.
