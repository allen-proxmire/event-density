## Genre conventions

Every paper follows the same 10-section template:

1. Framing
2. Claim (one sentence)
3. Scope (FORCED / INHERITED / OUT OF SCOPE)
4. Key vocabulary
5. Substrate class $\{C\}$
6. Alternative encodings (A-series structural, B-series mainstream)
7. Constructive necessity
8. Exclusion arguments (with summary table)
9. Falsifiers and empirical exposure
10. Appendix: derivation chain + glossary

Each paper is **standalone** and cold-reader accessible; no knowledge of ED or prior papers in the series is assumed, though downstream papers take upstream results as substrate-class inputs.
