## §2 Three readings of post-boundary phase structure

**Reading X — two distinct phase references.** $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$ is set independently by post-boundary substrate (per Memo_ED_V5PhaseContent §3 — "no substrate-graph content forces $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}} = \pi_K^{\mathrm{pre}}$ across the boundary"). **Reading Y — single effective reference via V1 cross-boundary transport.** $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$ is itself derived from V1-transported content across SCBU. Under this reading, $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}} = \pi_K^{\mathrm{pre}}$ (or differs only by a global $U(1)$ choice equivalent to the pre-boundary global choice), and V5-delivery is structurally redundant with $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$.

**Reading Z — intermediate, V1-active content distinction.** $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$ exists as the post-boundary substrate's "background" phase reference, but V5 cross-boundary content is also "alive" at post-boundary loci as substrate content that V1 is actively propagating forward. New chains created at V1-active loci adopt the V1-active content as their initial phase because V1 is the substrate's forward-causal mechanism and commitment events are V1-coupled.

Each reading has different substrate-graph implications for B3.

## §3 Reading X analysis

Two distinct phase references at each post-boundary locus. B3 asks why new chains inherit V5-delivered phase over $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$.

**Candidate substrate-graph arguments under Reading X:**

- *V1 retardation carries history.* V5-delivered phase has substrate-graph history (V1-transported across SCBU); $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$ is fresh (no history). If commitment events preferentially align with content carrying history, V5-delivered phase wins.
- *V1 retardation has substrate-c bound.* V1-transported content propagates at substrate-c (per Paper_012 P-RB-1 + Paper_089). $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$ is whatever the substrate locus carries instantaneously. If commitment events couple to active V1-transported content rather than to instantaneous substrate state, V5-delivery wins.

**Status:** Both candidate arguments require a substrate-graph principle — *"commitment events preferentially align with V1-transported content"* — that **is not currently in the corpus.** The corpus does not specify how commitment events choose among available phase references when multiple are present at a locus.

**Reading X under B3 → OPEN.** Same kind of inheritance gap as CommitPhaseInheritance Path-A faced. No substrate-graph closure from existing primitives.
