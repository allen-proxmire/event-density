## Weak Link 1 — Channel-uniqueness vs spatial homogeneity

**Question:** Does any corpus content force "only one P09 channel per kernel-arrow direction at each locus" (channel-uniqueness), beyond what spatial homogeneity (Paper_ED_CCC §3.6 + SC-4.x) supplies?

**Closure likelihood: LOW.** Three structural reasons:
- Paper_ED_SC_4_9 saddle Hessian explicitly has multiple eigenvalue-axes per locus (compression-dominant + expansion-dominant); the substrate-graph framework is *already* multi-channel by construction.
- Memo_ED_ChainArrowChirality §3.3 presupposes $\pi_C$ and $\pi_K$ can differ — channel-uniqueness directly contradicts this presupposition.
- Standard-physics intuition: even a homogeneous vacuum has multiple field sectors at each spacetime point. The corpus's substrate-ontology lineage (Wolfram, 't Hooft, causal-set) does not naturally restrict to single-channel-per-locus.

**Arc-movement value: HIGH if it closes.** Would directly supply Step 7 of CommitPhaseInheritance → asymmetric admission D-via-I → baryogenesis M2 → M3.

**Contradiction risk: HIGH.** Directly conflicts with the chirality memo's own definition. If "channel-uniqueness" were forced by primitives, the chirality $\chi_C \in S^1$ definition would collapse to a single point — but the corpus uses it as a continuous variable. Investigating this question likely yields a clean negative result quickly.

## Weak Link 2 — Anti-aligned chain creation post-saturation

**Question:** Can the framework permit anti-aligned chain creation in post-saturation regimes without violating channel-uniqueness in saturation? Equivalently: is the CommitPhaseInheritance route internally consistent with the observed existence of antimatter?

**Closure likelihood: MEDIUM, but the path forward depends on what closes.** Two sub-paths:
- (a) Regime-dependent channel structure — substrate has more channels available post-saturation than in saturation. Requires substrate-graph derivation of why capacity-relief opens new channels. Plausible but unconstructed.
- (b) Abandon channel-uniqueness; find a different asymmetric-admission mechanism. Requires reconsidering the entire kernel-arrow route.

**Arc-movement value: HIGH because either outcome is informative.**
- Positive closure (a) supplies regime-dependent substrate machinery that may have applications beyond baryogenesis.
- Negative outcome (b) confirms the CommitPhaseInheritance route is structurally limited and redirects effort to alternatives (V5-cross-boundary, T17 bundle, P05 holonomy).

**Contradiction risk: MEDIUM.** This is the *diagnostic* question for the kernel-arrow route — it tests whether the route is salvageable at all. Outcome may force acknowledgment that the route is structurally bounded.

**Note:** Weak Link 2 is partially dependent on Weak Link 1 and Weak Link 3 outcomes. Until those settle, the "channel-uniqueness in saturation" premise (a) tries to preserve isn't clearly defined.

## Weak Link 3 — V5 cross-boundary memory P09 phase content

**Question:** Does V5 cross-boundary memory content (Paper_090 + Paper_ED_CCC §3.4 twistor-mass-conservation identification) carry P09 phase information at substrate-graph level?

**Closure likelihood: MEDIUM-HIGH.** This is the most concrete substrate-graph question of the three — a definite yes/no fact about V5's structural content. Examining Paper_090 + Paper_ED_CCC §3.4 + V5's role in cross-chain memory should yield a clear answer. The investigation is well-defined regardless of which way it lands.

**Arc-movement value: VARIABLE, but high under either outcome.**
- **If V5 has NO P09 phase content:** removes one objection to the CommitPhaseInheritance route (V5 doesn't supply alternative substrate-side phase references). Partially helps Path-A but doesn't supply the load-bearing channel-uniqueness step.
- **If V5 HAS P09 phase content:** kills Path-A directly (alternative phase references exist; channel-uniqueness fails), but **opens a new substrate-graph route** — V5 cross-boundary as carrier of pre-aeon chirality preference. This is Angle 2 from Memo_ED_Baryogenesis_NextExplorations, and it ties baryogenesis into the CCC cross-aeon framing in a substantively new way.

**Contradiction risk: LOW.** The investigation is well-defined; no structural tension; clean outcome either way.

## Non-prescriptive observations

A few structural observations on natural ordering, without recommending one:

- **WL3 is the most concrete to investigate** (definite substrate-graph fact about V5; low contradiction risk; either outcome informative). It also touches the CCC arc, so closure ripples beyond baryogenesis (Angle 2 from NextExplorations).
- **WL1 is structurally the most likely to yield a clean negative quickly** because it conflicts with the chirality memo presupposition. A short focused memo could settle it negatively in ~1500 words and free up effort.
- **WL2 is hardest to attack directly** because its premise ("channel-uniqueness in saturation, multi-channel post-saturation") isn't clearly defined until WL1 and WL3 settle. Its value as a diagnostic comes *after* the other two.
- **WL3 has the unique property of potentially opening a new substrate-graph route** even when it forecloses the current one. If V5 carries phase content, the baryogenesis arc gains a CCC-cross-aeon mechanism candidate (Angle 2) that's structurally independent of CommitPhaseInheritance — and that route hasn't been explored.
- **None of the three guarantees baryogenesis M2 → M3.** All three are substrate-research-frontier investigations whose outcomes may be informative without being conclusive.

A pragmatic reading: WL3 first if you want concrete movement (yes/no fact + possible new route opening); WL1 first if you want to clear the table quickly (likely negative; closes one option fast); WL2 only after the others settle.

A research-program reading: all three are honest substrate-research frontier work. Closure of any one updates the corpus's understanding of what the substrate can support; failure to close updates equally informatively. The baryogenesis arc at M2 stands as the honest stopping point regardless of which is pursued — none of these is a blocker for moving on to other ED territory (PDFs / Zenodo / other arcs).

The four-memo chirality cascade arrived at honest M2 with one postulate; the kernel-arrow route has not improved on that. Further investigation has positive expected value (especially WL3) but is not load-bearing for the corpus's overall coherence. Whether to pursue it is appetite-driven, not necessity-driven.
