## §3 Promising conceptual angles

Five angles worth holding open during further exploration. None claimed to close; all worth a focused memo if pursued.

**Angle 1: Kernel-arrow as a natural symmetry-breaker for asymmetric admission.**
Per Paper_089 T18, V1 retarded propagation has a unique forward direction (advanced V1 refuted by P11). The substrate kernel-arrow is *directional* at substrate-graph level. A chain with $\chi_C = 0$ (P09 phase aligned with kernel-arrow's P09 phase) is "in phase" with the substrate's V1 retarded support; a chain with $\chi_C = \pi$ is $\pi$-shifted. In the saturation regime where substrate capacity is exhausted, maintaining the $\pi$-phase relationship continuously during V1 transport may itself impose an additional substrate-graph cost that the aligned chain does not incur. This is the substrate-graph version of "matter is in step with substrate flow; antimatter is out of step." It directly addresses (b) asymmetric admission cost without requiring (a) binary admission closure first. **Worth a focused memo.**

**Angle 2: V5 cross-chain coupling as a baryogenesis carrier.**
V5 (Paper_090) supplies cross-chain finite-memory coupling. In the saturation regime, V5 correlations are maximally constrained. Two sub-questions: (i) does V5 prefer coupling between same-chirality chains over opposite-chirality chains (substrate-graph chirality-symmetry-breaking via V5)? (ii) Does V5 cross-boundary memory at the SCBU boundary (Paper_ED_CCC §3.4 twistor mass-conservation identification) inherit a chirality preference from the pre-boundary substrate? Sub-question (ii) connects baryogenesis to CCC's cross-aeon mass-conservation in a substantively new way. **Speculative but interesting.**

**Angle 3: Connection to T1 spin-statistics $\mathbb{Z}_2$.**
Paper_098_5 / T1 establishes that $D = 3+1$ forces exactly two statistics classes (boson, fermion). This is already a substrate-side $\mathbb{Z}_2$ structure forced by dimension. The standard physics framing: matter / antimatter distinction is connected to charge conjugation $C$; spin-statistics is connected to particle-exchange under $C$. **Is T1's $\mathbb{Z}_2$ related to chirality $\mathbb{Z}_2$?** Probably independent structures, but the corpus has not explicitly checked. Cheap to verify via focused memo. Could supply a surprising substrate-graph $\mathbb{Z}_2$ derivation if related.

**Angle 4: Saddle-Hessian eigenvalue asymmetry under V1 propagation.**
Per Paper_ED_SC_4_9, saddle Hessian has both positive and negative eigenvalues. Per Paper_ED_GW_00, V1 retarded propagation carries the Hessian-signature pattern outward. In the saturation regime, expansion-dominant Hessian axes are sustained while compression-dominant are suppressed (per Paper_ED_Cos_01 §3.3 inflation reading). **Question:** does this Hessian-eigenvalue asymmetry under V1 propagation produce a directional preference at the chirality level? If aligned-chirality couples to expansion-dominant axes preferentially (or some similar structural preference), the chirality asymmetry inherits from the Hessian asymmetry. Speculative connection; would tie baryogenesis to the inflation arc structurally.

**Angle 5: Asymmetric admission may not require binary admission as prerequisite.**
The four-memo cascade treated (a) binary admission as the prerequisite for (b) asymmetric admission. But the dependency may run the other way: in a substrate where (b) supplies a directional preference (e.g., via kernel-arrow per Angle 1), the substrate may *only* admit one chirality class — making the substrate effectively monary in the saturation regime. Under this reading, "antimatter" doesn't exist at the substrate level in the saturation regime; it only emerges post-saturation when production has caught up to diffusion. **This reframes the question.** Worth holding open as an alternative reading.
