## §5 Cosmology-arc integration

This is the section where the modern architecture (SCBU + CCC + SC-4.x) significantly tightens ED-I-11's informal framing. Five integration points:

### 5.1 Saturation regime ↔ SCBU boundary

ED-I-11's "saturated ED-flow" is the **opposite-extreme** of the SCBU boundary: SCBU lives at extreme dilution (event-density → 0), saturation lives at the upper end (event-density at structural maximum). Both are **substrate phase boundaries** at the cosmic scale. Per Paper_ED_CCC §3.1 + §3.7, the substrate cosmic phase structure contains:

- **Pre-boundary regime:** late-aeon dilution; matter content negligible; V1 + V5 dominant (the GWE analog).
- **SCBU boundary:** substrate-cosmology surface; conformal-flatness/scale-collapse content; horizon-reset.
- **Post-boundary ignition regime:** P12 threshold re-entry from below; substrate event-density climbs rapidly; V1 + V5 + Paper_072 individuation reactivate.
- **Saturation regime (ED-I-11 candidate):** event-density approaches upper structural threshold; admission filter selective; this is the substrate-side reading of the "hot Big Bang" / Radiation-Dominated Epoch onset.
- **Standard individuation regime:** capacity > demand; symmetric admission; current substrate state.

**Identification:** the early-universe saturation regime is structurally placed as the **immediate post-SCBU ignition phase** within Paper_ED_CCC §3.7. It is not a separate cosmic phase but a quantitative subregion of the ignition phase characterized by capacity-near-demand.

This integration is structurally clean: baryogenesis is the consequence of the substrate cosmic phase structure already named in the corpus, not a new mechanism.

### 5.2 Tension polarity ↔ kernel-arrow asymptotics in the early universe

Post-SCBU ignition, the substrate inherits the boundary's scale-collapsed (SC-4.x) globally-homogeneous content. The kernel-arrow (Paper_093 T18) emerges from this boundary state with a **globally-coherent direction** at cosmic scale: no local content existed pre-boundary to break the homogeneity, so the kernel-arrow is uniform across the post-boundary substrate.

In this regime, "tension polarity" (chain-arrow vs kernel-arrow alignment) becomes binary: either a chain's V1-direction aligns with the globally-coherent kernel-arrow, or it doesn't. The local relaxation direction has no local freedom that an anti-aligned chain could occupy.

**Substrate identification:** tension polarity in the early-universe regime is determined by the post-SCBU kernel-arrow asymptotic. Once construction (a) closes — chain-arrow chirality as substrate-graph quantity — the early-universe expression of (a) is a direct consequence of the post-boundary kernel-arrow homogeneity, not an additional mechanism.

This is structurally tighter than ED-I-11's informal "phase-aligned vs phase-shifted" language: the post-boundary kernel-arrow homogeneity supplies the substrate-side reason why the alignment is binary at this stage.

### 5.3 V1 / V5 asymptotics and the natural saturation mechanism

In the post-SCBU ignition regime, V1 retarded propagation (Paper_089) and V5 cross-chain memory (Paper_090) reactivate from the boundary's kernel-content-only state. As event-density climbs:

- V1 finite-width retarded support is increasingly committed to coherent propagation of accumulating chain content.
- V5 cross-chain correlations are increasingly constrained by capacity at each chain-pair locus.
- Both kernels approach their structural maximum participation density.

**Construction (b) candidate.** Update-capacity saturation can be naturally identified with the regime where V1's finite-width support + P04 bandwidth × P07 channel multiplicity at every locus is at admission demand — i.e., no slack exists for substrate rearrangement at any locus. This is a quantitative substrate scale derivable in principle from V1 finite-width parameters (Paper_089) + P04 / P07 substrate content + Paper_092 $\xi_{canonical}$ scale-correspondence (Route A OPEN).

The natural saturation mechanism is therefore **already implicit in V1 + V5 asymptotics + P04/P07 capacity content**, not requiring new primitives. The work needed: derive the saturation threshold quantitatively. This is the kind of construction the SC-series arc is appropriate for.

### 5.4 CCC GWE epoch vs ED's high-ED regime — structural distinction

The Meissner–Penrose 2025 Gravitational Wave Epoch (Paper_ED_CCC §3.3) is **not** the same regime as ED-I-11's saturation. The GWE is the **late-aeon dilution** phase, characterized by matter-content-negligible and Weyl-curvature-dominant — that is, kernel-dominated at low event-density. ED-I-11's saturation is the **early-aeon high event-density** phase, characterized by matter-content-emergent and admission-capacity-saturated.

**Structural analogy (not identity):** both regimes have **globally-coherent kernel-arrow content at cosmic scale**. GWE achieves this via Weyl-dominance after dilution erases local content; the post-SCBU ignition / saturation regime achieves this via boundary-inherited scale-collapse before local content has accumulated. **Same cosmic-scale kernel-arrow property, opposite ends of the substrate phase structure.**

This dual-symmetric placement is itself structurally interesting: the substrate cosmic phase structure has kernel-arrow homogeneity at both extremes (just-pre-SCBU and just-post-SCBU) with normal individuation content in the middle. ED-I-11's chirality-selection mechanism applies at the post-SCBU end; whether the analog applies at the pre-SCBU GWE end is a question worth flagging but is OPEN.

### 5.5 Baryogenesis as boundary-selection effect

Under §5.1–5.4, baryogenesis acquires a structural placement: it is the **direct downstream consequence of post-SCBU substrate homogeneity** combined with **construction (a) chain-arrow chirality**. Specifically:

- Post-SCBU substrate inherits a globally-coherent kernel-arrow from scale-collapse (Paper_ED_CCC §3.6 + §3.7).
- The first chains to emerge above P12 threshold encounter a substrate with no local kernel-arrow freedom.
- Any chain attempting admission with chirality anti-aligned to the global kernel-arrow fails the substrate admission filter (Paper_072 individuation criterion fails because no local substrate content can support the phase-shifted update).
- As event-density climbs through the saturation regime, the asymmetric admission persists until capacity > demand and local kernel-arrow freedom returns.
- Post-saturation: standard symmetric pair-production resumes.

**This is a stronger framing than ED-I-11's standalone narrative.** Baryogenesis is not a separate mechanism — it is a structural consequence of the post-SCBU boundary content already in the corpus, combined with one OPEN construction (chain-arrow chirality). **Baryogenesis is a boundary-selection effect in the same structural family as the SCBU horizon-reset and the CCC Hawking-spot anchor.**

The eventual baryogenesis paper is therefore naturally a **cosmology-arc paper** anchored to Paper_ED_CCC §3.7, not a standalone post-Standard-Model paper.
