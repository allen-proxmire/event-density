## §1 Restate the mechanism

Angle 2 (from Memo_ED_Baryogenesis_NextExplorations) proposes a substrate-graph mechanism for asymmetric admission distinct from the kernel-arrow route audited in Memo_ED_CommitPhaseInheritance_Audit:

- **Pre-boundary aeon** had a chirality distribution among its chains. If pre-aeon saturation regime produced matter-dominance (by some mechanism), pre-aeon substrate carried a *majority phase* across its chain population — the P09 phase content associated with the dominant chirality class.
- **V5 cross-boundary memory** at SCBU carries phase-bearing content from pre-aeon substrate across the boundary (per Memo_ED_V5PhaseContent + Paper_ED_CCC §3.4).
- **Post-boundary substrate** in the ignition regime has access to V5-delivered majority phase content as an alternative substrate-side phase reference distinct from $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$.
- **Asymmetric admission** emerges if post-boundary new-chain commitment events preferentially inherit V5-delivered majority phase rather than $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$ — biasing the new-chain population toward the pre-aeon dominant chirality class.

The mechanism distributes the baryogenesis explanation across the SCBU boundary: each aeon inherits its chirality preference from the prior. CCC-cyclic baryogenesis.

The substrate-graph derivations required:

- **(a)** Encoding of pre-aeon majority phase in V5 cross-boundary content.
- **(b)** Preferential inheritance of V5-delivered phase by post-boundary new-chain commitment events.
- **(c)** Cross-aeon phase content matching dominant chirality (not necessarily $\pi$-shifted; see §4 below).
- **(d)** First-aeon initial chirality question (or cyclic infinite-regress).

## §3 Sub-derivation (b) — Preferential inheritance at post-boundary new-chain commitment

**This is the load-bearing OPEN derivation.** Post-boundary substrate at each locus has access to two phase references: $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$ (the post-boundary kernel-arrow phase) and V5-delivered $\pi_K^{\mathrm{pre}}$ (the pre-aeon kernel-arrow phase carried across SCBU). New-chain commitment events at the locus must inherit one of these (or some weighted combination) as the new chain's initial P09 phase $\pi_C(e_{\mathrm{new}})$.

**The substrate-graph question:** is there any mechanism in the existing corpus that forces (or strongly biases) new-chain commitments to inherit V5-delivered phase over $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$?

**Candidate arguments:**

**Candidate B1 — Substrate-graph "weight" via cross-chain correlation density.** V5 cross-boundary content is the substrate-side memory of pre-boundary chain-pair structure. Post-boundary substrate in the ignition regime is freshly populating; new chains coupling via V5 to existing V5-cross-boundary content would naturally adopt the V5-delivered phase content. Under this reading: new chains created with V5-cross-chain coupling content are biased toward V5-delivered phase.

Status: requires substrate-graph derivation of how cross-chain coupling at commitment events biases initial phase assignment. **Not constructed in the corpus.** Plausible but speculative.

**Candidate B2 — Substrate-graph "history weight" via V5 finite memory.** V5's finite-memory kernel structure means cross-boundary content has accumulated substrate-side history (pre-aeon substrate's full V5 history is encoded in the cross-boundary memory). $\pi_K^{\mathrm{post}}$ is "fresh" (post-boundary substrate just emerged). Substrate-graph commitment events might preferentially align with substrate content carrying more history.

Status: requires a substrate-graph principle that "history weight" affects commitment-phase assignment. **Not currently in the corpus.** Speculative — would be a paper-specific postulate if declared.

**Candidate B3 — Substrate-graph "directional weight" via Paper_093 T18.** Per Paper_093 T18, the kernel-arrow is the V1 retarded-support direction. V5 cross-boundary content was transported via V1 retarded propagation across the boundary (per Paper_ED_CCC §3.4 ψ-spinor identification with V1-propagated content). Therefore V5-delivered phase is the substrate-side phase content that "arrived via V1 retardation." Post-boundary new chains created with V1-coupled content would inherit this V1-retardation-transported phase.

Status: requires substrate-graph derivation that V1-transported phase content has preferential weight at commitment events over locally-available phase content. **Not currently in the corpus.** Plausible from V1's load-bearing role but not derived.

**Verdict on (b):** all three candidate arguments require substrate-graph derivations that don't close from existing primitives. **(b) is OPEN at substrate-graph level.**

This is structurally parallel to the CommitPhaseInheritance situation: the inheritance step is load-bearing and not derivable from existing primitives. The V5-cross-boundary route faces the same kind of inheritance gap as the kernel-arrow route did.

## §4 Reframing (c) — cross-aeon phase difference

The earlier framing assumed the cross-aeon phase difference must be specifically $\pi$ (anti-aligned) for V5 cross-boundary to deliver "antimatter-class" content. **This was a misreading.**

Under the corrected Angle 2 reading: V5 cross-boundary carries the *dominant* pre-aeon phase content, which is the matter-class phase (by hypothesis: pre-aeon had matter-dominance). Post-boundary new chains inheriting this phase content become **matter chains**, not antimatter. The asymmetry mechanism is "matter-class phase content delivered by V5 → preferential matter creation in post-aeon," not "V5 delivers $\pi$-shifted phase → antimatter."

This is structurally cleaner. The cross-aeon phase difference need not be specifically $\pi$; the V5-delivered content just needs to be the majority pre-aeon chirality phase, whatever its absolute value in $S^1$.

**Implication:** the V5-cross-boundary mechanism doesn't require a specific $\pi$-shift relationship between aeons. It just requires V5 to deliver majority-phase content + post-boundary new chains to preferentially inherit it. The first requirement closes per §2; the second is OPEN per §3.

This reframing slightly *strengthens* the mechanism's structural plausibility — no specific phase-alignment between aeons needs to be derived; just majority-content preservation across the boundary.

Status: requires a separate substrate-graph mechanism for first-aeon chirality preference. Not currently in the corpus. Would be a substrate-research-frontier substantively different from Angle 2.
